---
title: Tank Arena
---

Tanks, helicopters and planes on top-down maps: missions against the computer, and two players
together or against each other. The rewrite runs on Linux, Windows, macOS and Android, and plays in
two ways:

- **Classic**: the original game, as it was. Same rules, same menus in the original's 320×200 look,
  same keys.
- **Next Kombat**: the same game with what has been added since. Keyboard and mouse, gamepads, up to
  four players, network games over the local network or the internet, replays and a map editor.

Builds will be on the [downloads](downloads/) page.
